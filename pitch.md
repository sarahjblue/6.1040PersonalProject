# Application Pitch


## Name: Infinite Clothing

The meaning behind this name is that there seems to be infinite clothing that the app aims to keep in infinite use, rather than allowing the clothing to be used and discarded.


## Motivation:
Infinite Reuse will address the lack of an easy, efficient, and consistently accessible way to buy, sell, or recycle clothing for students at MIT by creating an online sales application where students can buy and sell their clothing items to fellow students at MIT.

## Key Features: what the feature is; why it helps mitigate the problem; and how it impacts stakeholders


### Uploading Items to sell

Users can upload their own items to sell. These uploaded items are associated with their account and stay available for all buyers until the item is sold. This keeps the items consistently accessible for students. The application can be utilized at any time. Since users upload each item independently, there is no reason to feel driven to improperly dispose of one item because it is "only one item". This impacts the environment because it encourages more clothing resale and reuse.


### Filtered Search

Users can utilize the search function to find items that match more specific requirements that the user is looking for such as size, color, or item type. This helps mitigate the challenges of clothing resale on campus by making searching for clothing both easier and efficient. Users can immediately narrow their search to clothing in their size, saving them time that would otherwise be invested in sifting through mass emails or at thrift stores.


### In-app Messaging

Users can message one another about an item that they are interested in purchasing. This impacts students by giving them a direct path of communication that is solely related to the process of selling and buying clothing. This messaging feature helps sellers maintain contact with buyers who are interested in their items. By condensing any and all clothing resale communications to be included in the app, it centralizes the process and prevents sellers from losing track of messages. It also makes it easier for buyers to check messages they have sent and maintain separate communications.
