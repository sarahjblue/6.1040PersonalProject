# Application Pitch


## Name: Infinite Clothing

The meaning behind this name is that there seems to be infinite clothing that the app aims to keep in infinite use, rather than allowing the clothing to be used and discarded. It is also a play on the Infinite Corridor, a well-known and loved landmark of MIT.


## Motivation:
Infinite Clothing will address the lack of an easy, efficient, and consistently accessible way to buy and sell clothing for students at MIT. This will be addressed by creating an online sales application where students can buy and sell their clothing items to fellow students at MIT.

## Key Features: what the feature is; why it helps mitigate the problem; and how it impacts stakeholders

### MIT Verification

Users will have to verify their MIT student status as part of their account. This will allow users access to Infinite Clothing and provide them with access to the site. Otherwise, users will be unable to utilize the web application.

### Easy Item Uploading

Users can upload their own items to sell. The upload process is easy, requiring only a picture. Beyond the picture, the user will be offered button options to provide more information for buyers about things such as size and color. These uploaded items are associated with their account and stay available for all buyers until the item is sold. This keeps the items consistently accessible for students. The application can be utilized at any time. This impacts the environment because it encourages more clothing resale and reuse.


### Filtered Search

Users can utilize the search function to find items that match more specific requirements that the user is looking for such as size, color, or item type. This helps mitigate the challenges of clothing resale on campus by making searching for clothing both easier and efficient. Users can immediately narrow their search to clothing in their size, saving them time that would otherwise be invested in sifting through mass emails or at thrift stores.


### In-app Private Messaging That Deletes when an Item has been Sold

Users can message one another about an item that they are interested in purchasing. The messages are tied to the item that is for sale, and after the item has been sold for more than 24 hours the conversation deletes itself. This impacts students by giving them a direct path of communication that is solely related to the process of selling and buying clothing. This messaging feature helps sellers maintain contact with buyers who are interested in their items. By condensing any and all clothing resale communications to be included in the app, it centralizes the process and prevents sellers from losing track of messages. It also makes it easier for buyers to check messages they have sent and maintain separate communications.
