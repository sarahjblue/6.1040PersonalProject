# Application Pitch


## Name: Infinite Clothing

The meaning behind this name is that there seems to be infinite clothing that the app aims to keep in infinite use, rather than allowing the clothing to be used and discarded. It is also a play on the Infinite Corridor, a well-known and loved landmark of MIT.


## Motivation:
Infinite Clothing will address the lack of an easy, efficient, and consistently accessible way to buy and sell clothing for students and other members of the MIT community (from here on out, students will be used, but also includes other MIT community members). This will be addressed by creating an online sales application where students can buy and sell their clothing items to fellow students at MIT.

## Key Features

### Easy Item Uploading

Users can upload their own items to sell, creating an online marketplace designed for MIT community members. The upload process is easy, requiring only a name and an image, with an optional description and price parameter. Beyond the picture, the user will be offered button options to provide more information for buyers about things such as size and color. These uploaded items are associated with their account and stay available for all buyers until the item is sold. The impact of easy item uploading is most felt by sellers. By reducing friction to uploading items, sellers are more likely to participate and use the product. The persistent marketplace online also keeps items consistently available for buyers, if a student wants to do some late night shopping, they can. By impacting both the buyers and the sellers, the environmental impact can also be felt as users resell and buy used clothing rather than disposing of it.


### Filtered Search

Users can utilize the search function to find items that match more specific requirements that the user is looking for such as size, color, or item type. This helps mitigate the challenges of clothing resale on campus by making searching for clothing both easier and efficient. It saves buyers time and energy that might have otherwise been spent sifting through mass emails or at clothing racks in thrift stores. Instead, users can immediately narrow their search to clothing in their size.


### In-app Private Messaging That Deletes when an Item has been Sold

Users can message one another about an item that they are interested in purchasing. The messages are tied to the item that is for sale, and after the item has been sold for more than 24 hours the conversation deletes itself. This impacts students by giving them a direct path of communication that is solely related to the process of selling and buying clothing. This messaging feature helps sellers maintain contact with buyers who are interested in their items. By condensing any and all clothing resale communications to be included in the app, it centralizes the process and prevents sellers from losing track of messages. It also makes it easier for buyers to check messages they have sent and maintain separate communications.
